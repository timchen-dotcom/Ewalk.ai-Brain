\f0\fs24 \cf0 # Brand DNA: \uc0\u38867 \u39135 \u26085 \u24120 \u37707 \u29289  (Hansik Ilsang Hotpot)\
\
## Core Philosophy\
- **Concept**: "Korean Soul, Taiwanese Hotpot Skeleton".\
- **Goal**: Transform Korean food from a "low-frequency, premium gathering" to a "high-frequency, affordable daily necessity".\
- **Target Audience**: Students (price-sensitive but highly viral), Young Professionals, and Families in Tainan (initial market).\
\
## Strategic Positioning: The "Downward Strike" (\uc0\u38477 \u32173 \u25171 \u25802 )\
- **Price Point**: \
  - Main meals (Bibimbap/Rice): 110 - 140 TWD.\
  - Soups/Hotpots: 180 - 260 TWD.\
- **Value Proposition**: Compete directly with Taiwanese affordable hotpot giants (like \uc0\u20845 \u25159 \u38272 , \u20740 \u21697 \u37707 ) by offering an equivalent price but a deeply distinct *Korean cultural experience*.\
- **Visual & Spatial Aesthetics**: Reject traditional dark/greasy Korean BBQ vibes. Use bright, light-industrial design, faux-marble tables, and authentic Korean brassware/pots to create a "premium feel" at a cheap price.}
