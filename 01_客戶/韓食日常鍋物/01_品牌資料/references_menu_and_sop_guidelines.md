\f0\fs24 \cf0 # Menu & SOP Guidelines\
\
## Menu Structure Strategy\
- **Modularity**: High ingredient sharing rate. (e.g., Zucchini, bean sprouts, carrots shared across all Bibimbap; Cabbage, potatoes, tofu shared across Doenjang Jjigae and Pork Rib Soup).\
- **Hero Products**: \
  - *Volume driver*: Pork/Beef BBQ Rice (110-120 TWD).\
  - *Margin & Core*: Pork Kimchi Pot (180 TWD).\
  - *Visual Viral driver*: Potato Pork Rib Soup (260 TWD) - must have explosive visual meat portions.\
\
## The Micro-Buffet strategy (Crucial for Scaling)\
- Do NOT serve pre-plated side dishes (too labor-intensive).\
- Implement a self-service bar containing:\
  - 5-6 classic, easy-to-store Korean side dishes (Kimchi, Pickled Radish, Seaweed).\
  - **Carb Upgrade**: Offer Korean Seaweed Sesame Rice or simple Tteokbokki instead of Taiwanese Braised Pork Rice (\uc0\u28407 \u32905 \u39151 ). This maintains the Korean DNA while satisfying the extreme demand for fullness in the affordable hotpot sector.\
\
## Operations\
- **Automation**: Fully utilize self-ordering Kiosks and QR-code mobile ordering to minimize front-of-house labor.\
- **Preparation**: Tableware (tissues, tongs, spoons) must be stored in hidden table drawers to clear table space and reduce staff refill trips.}
